How the numbers are calculated
Strip count
Fewest full-width strips that still meet the minimum overlap: n = ceil((L − omin) / (w − omin)), for face length L and roll width w.
Actual overlap
The minimum-strip overlap is then spread evenly across every seam: o = (n·w − L) / (n − 1) — always ≥ the spec minimum, never a trimmed strip.
Roll off-cut
Strips are pooled across all lifts that share the same embedment length, then packed into 1×roll-length pieces (floor(roll length ÷ embedment)) to minimise leftover per roll.
